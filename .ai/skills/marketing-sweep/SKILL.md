# Marketing sweep (Brian's default)

Account: brian.tubergen@gmail.com.

## Scope it to the inbox

Default to `in:inbox` only. His Promotions category is full of years of already-archived mail; a category-wide scan produces a long list of senders he dealt with long ago and wastes his time. If he wants a fuller sweep, he'll say so.

## What counts

Pure marketing: newsletters, retail promos, drip campaigns, event blasts.

Never include: order/shipping mail, receipts, statements, security alerts, travel confirmations, fund and investor mail (NAV Fund Services, AngelList), anything from an actual person.

Flag separately rather than lumping in - he decides these case by case:
- cold sales outreach from named humans
- investor/professional invites
- his pharmacy (NimbleRx)
- vendor mail that mixes marketing with transactional alerts (ether.fi is the example: `updates@ether.fi` is marketing, `info@ether.fi` alerts are not)

## Report format he likes

Grouped by sender, ranked by volume: **name - from address - count - typical subject**. Flag which have a working unsubscribe path and which don't. Then he picks.

Do not unsubscribe from anything before he picks.

## Doing the unsubscribes

Most unsubscribe links need a real confirmation click - Mailchimp, Mailjet, preference centers. Use a browser, click through, and read the confirmation page for each one. Report what each page said. Don't report success from sending a request alone.

Watch for:
- preference centers: choose "unsubscribe from all marketing", not one list
- Luma: leave "decline all pending invites" unchecked
- Google Ads rep mail: opting out stops the automated stream, the rep can still email directly
- list subscriptions under a plus-alias (Tao Group had him as brian.tubergen+2@gmail.com) - worth telling him

## Then archive

**Always list sender + subject and wait for his explicit confirmation before archiving anything.** He stated this directly. The audit list and his picks cover the unsubscribes; archiving is its own confirmation.

Once he confirms: unsubscribing only stops future mail, so archive the messages already sitting there, in one batch. They stay in All Mail.
