# Inbox sweep (Brian's default)

Account: brian.tubergen@gmail.com. Scope `in:inbox` unless he says otherwise.

Runs all three sweeps in one pass:

1. **marketing-sweep** - promos, newsletters, event blasts. Unsubscribes, then archives.
2. **payments-sweep** - money in/out, scheduled payments, paid invoices. Labels 4_Payments, then archives.
3. **one-off-sweep** - sign-in links, 2FA codes, verifications. Archives only, never unsubscribes.

Read each of those SKILL.md files and follow their inclusion, exclusion, and gotcha rules exactly. They own the judgment; this skill owns the sequencing.

## How to present it

Classify the whole inbox once, then give him **one message with three sections**, not three separate reports. Sender + subject + date per item, arguable calls flagged in their own group.

**Nothing is archived, labeled, or unsubscribed until he confirms.** He can approve section by section or all at once; take whatever he gives.

Anything that fits none of the three categories stays in the inbox and doesn't need mentioning individually - a one-line count is enough.

## Reporting back

After his go: what was unsubscribed (with confirmation-page results), what got labeled 4_Payments, what was archived, and the inbox count before and after.
